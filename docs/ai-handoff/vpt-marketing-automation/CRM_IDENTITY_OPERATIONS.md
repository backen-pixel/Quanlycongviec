# Company identity review — CRM_EXACT_CONTACT_REVIEW_V1

Base PR22: 301db0d720c7fb2f84ef6b1d2c9a7c4850a76703. Additive migration654; implementation and isolated verification only. Risk HIGH: identity decisions affect future customer counts; company-wide locking requires load rehearsal before release. No production changes or budget execution.

## Behavior and scope

The system scans all current CRM Leads/Deals and retained identity history in the company, including unpaid, unqualified and out-of-trial records. Exact normalized contact matches create review candidates; they never merge customers automatically. It compares both Lead and Customer contacts. Vietnamese mobile/landline formats and explicit international numbers normalize conservatively; invalid or ambiguous contacts leave the group unresolved. Email normalization only trims and lowercases; dots and plus tags are preserved. This is an explicit exact-contact policy, not a claim that every person using different contact details can be discovered.

An authorized company admin or sales admin can confirm same customer, confirm separate customers sharing contact, unlink a direct edge, revoke a distinct decision, reconfirm an entire changed group or detach an unavailable historical member. Current scope, tenant, actor and source are checked in PostgreSQL before recording. Server derives identity/company from authenticated context. Each command includes a unique request, evidence and the whole inventory snapshot token. Company identity writes serialize; Lead rows, including historical moved IDs, are locked in stable order. Historical-node locks and an INSERT restore trigger prevent stale detach decisions when records return or are restored. Missing/moved members are shown without foreign personal data.

LINK cannot contradict an active DISTINCT, even through another member or through the older651 API. Stale DISTINCT requires explicit revocation or renewed confirmation; UNLINK alone does not mean two customers. RECONFIRM refreshes every active edge in the component and clears its review markers without resetting generations. An unavailable member can be detached from the graph, but its historical node, source and receipt evidence remain. Restoring an old ID increments its generation. Every action preserves CRM/customer rows and existing qualification/attribution evidence.

The projection recalculates on every read. New Leads, contact changes, stale decisions, moved/missing members or unresolved candidates invalidate the affected result. A complete inventory scan can still contain unresolved groups. A resolved group is not a qualified or paid Lead. `qualifiedUniquePaidLead` and `allowBudgetExecution` remain false. The existing651 projection retains its conservative behavior. Whole-company global completeness remains false while historical orphans remain unresolved; future trial reporting must retain and disposition source/receipt tombstones explicitly rather than discard them.

## Operator UI and API

GET/POST `/crm/leads/:id/identity-review` is mounted behind CRM authentication/access middleware, primary-only and default-off `VPT_CRM_IDENTITY_REVIEW=1`. The service uses private SQL RPCs; public/direct service table access is revoked. Read responses omit raw phone/email contacts. The Lead detail quality tab adds a lazy review card for admin-like users/sales admin; database authority remains decisive. Operators search company records, select a conclusion, enter evidence and acknowledge responsibility.

Failed reads clear old results. Scope changes discard late responses. Ambiguous writes retain the exact request/body and lock editing/reload until retry confirms the result; terminal permission/staleness errors require a fresh read. Retrying accepted requests returns current state and rechecks rights, never resurrects an old graph. A changed search clears a previously selected counterpart. The inventory is capped at5000 records, candidate group pairs at10000; exceeding either is an explicit failure, never a silently partial or zero report.
